Privacy Policy for SizeFit

Last updated: September 26, 2026

🛡️ 100% On-Device Processing: All photo compression and resizing occur strictly on your device. SizeFit never uploads, transmits, or stores your private photos on external servers.

This Privacy Policy describes how SizeFit ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your Photos and Media: Images you choose to compress or resize are processed strictly in your device's local memory. We never transmit, upload, inspect, copy, or store your photos on any external servers or cloud services.
  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): SizeFit does not require account creation, registration, or logins. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or payment details.

2. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To keep SizeFit free for everyone, the App integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements.

Third-party service providers like Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device and usage information in accordance with their own privacy policies. This information may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • Device model, operating system version, and screen resolution
  • IP address and approximate general location (country or city level)
  • Ad interaction metrics (e.g., impressions and clicks)

For more details on how Google processes advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Device Permissions

  • Photos / Media Access (Photo Picker): Required to allow you to select photos from your gallery for compression, and to save the resulting compressed photos back to your device storage.
  • Internet Permission (INTERNET): Required solely for Google AdMob to load banner advertisements.

4. Children's Privacy

SizeFit does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to third-party services through our App, please contact us, and we will take appropriate steps.

5. Security

Because all image compression and resizing operations are executed entirely on your local device, your photos never leave your hardware. Network requests made by third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob) utilize industry-standard HTTPS encryption.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
