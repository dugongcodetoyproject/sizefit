# Privacy Policy for SizeFit

**Last updated:** September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **SizeFit** ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. 

We are committed to protecting your privacy. SizeFit is designed with a **privacy-first architecture**: all photo compression and image resizing tasks are performed **100% locally on your device**.

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect

- **Your Photos and Media**: When you select an image to compress or resize, the image file is processed entirely within your device's local memory using local device algorithms. We **never** transmit, upload, inspect, copy, or store your photos on any external servers or cloud services.
- **Personal Identifiable Information (PII)**: SizeFit does not require account creation, registration, or logins. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or payment details.

---

## 2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To keep SizeFit free for all users, the App integrates **Google AdMob** to display advertisements (banner ads).

Third-party service providers like Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device and usage information in accordance with their own privacy policies. This information may include:
- Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / IDFA)
- Device model, operating system version, and screen resolution
- IP address and approximate general location (country or city level)
- Ad interaction and performance metrics (e.g., ad impressions and clicks)

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google's official privacy documentation:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

## 3. Device Permissions

SizeFit requests only the minimal device permissions required to perform its functions:

- **Photos / Media Access (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / Photo Picker)**: Required to allow you to select photos from your gallery for compression, and to save the resulting compressed photos back to your device storage.
- **Internet Permission (`INTERNET` / `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)**: Required solely for Google AdMob to load banner advertisements.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

SizeFit does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to third-party services through our App, please contact us, and we will take appropriate steps.

---

## 5. Security

Because all image processing happens strictly on your device, your private photos never leave your hardware. For any network requests handled by third-party SDKs (such as AdMob), industry-standard HTTPS encryption is utilized.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
